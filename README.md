# Zorp Talk

Phone to phone **without internet**: walkie-talkie, calls, messages, voice notes, songs and files,
over **Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct**. Android 10 or newer, with Google Play services.

## Download

**[Download ZorpTalk.apk](https://github.com/AmraniHub/zorp-talk-app/releases/latest/download/ZorpTalk.apk)** (20 MB)

The link always gives the newest version. Install it on **both** phones.

## Install

1. Open the downloaded **ZorpTalk.apk** and allow your browser or file manager to **install unknown apps**.
2. If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap **More details > Install anyway**.
3. Open Zorp Talk and tap **Allow** for each permission. Turn on Bluetooth and Wi-Fi when it asks
   (Wi-Fi does not need to join any network).
4. Open it on the other phone too. They find each other: the first time both show the same code,
   tap **Connect** on both. After that they connect by themselves.

## Use

- **Hold the big button to talk**, let go to finish. It plays out loud on the other phone right away.
- **Phone button**: a live two-way call.
- **Messages**, **voice messages** (hold the wave button), and **files, songs, photos** (clip button,
  or share to Zorp Talk from any app).

**Range:** about 100 m in the open over Wi-Fi Direct, 10 to 30 m over Bluetooth, less through walls.

On Xiaomi phones, allow **Autostart** and set Battery to **No restrictions** for Zorp Talk.
